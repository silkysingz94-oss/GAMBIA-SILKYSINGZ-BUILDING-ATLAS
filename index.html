10 / METHODOLOGY & LIMITATIONS

Building polygons are queried from OpenStreetMap using the visible map extent. The map shows buildings mapped in OSM, not a verified census of every structure in The Gambia. Missing footprints do not mean buildings are absent. Footprint area is calculated geodesically from polygon geometry. Building counts and area statistics refer only to currently loaded features. National completeness requires an authoritative building inventory or a processed country extract.

11 / DATA SOURCES

OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Administrative overlay: Gambia Census EA GeoJSON, where accessible. Basemaps: Esri, OpenStreetMap and CARTO. Always verify boundary and footprint suitability before official statistical use.
