10 / METHODOLOGY & LIMITATIONS

Building polygons are retrieved from OpenStreetMap using the visible map extent. The application uses multiple smaller geographic requests to improve retrieval reliability. The maximum display capacity is 50,000 features per session. Footprint area is calculated geodesically from polygon geometry. The results represent buildings mapped in OpenStreetMap, not a verified census of all structures in The Gambia. Missing footprints do not mean buildings are absent. National completeness requires a validated country-wide building inventory or processed national extract.
